# Billing contract v2: Standard, Compute, overage and session limits

Status: **pinned 2026-10-18** for request `os-billing-compute-20261018-v2` (PR #196 extension).
Ships **disabled** behind the existing v1 flags (`docs/billing/CONTRACT.md` §7). No new env var.
The executable source of truth is `app/src/server/billing/v2/contract.ts`; the arithmetic oracle is
`app/src/server/billing/v2/fixtures/golden.json`, generated by an independent script (not the implementation).
Where this document and v1 disagree, v2 wins for new offers; v1 entitlements that were already sold are honoured.

## 1. Offers (catalog v2)

| SKU | Price (before tax) | Included per window | Sizes | Overage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `free_v2` | $0 | 2 h per UTC month | `free-cf` (Cloudflare 2 vCPU / 6 GiB) | none |
| `free_v1_legacy` | $0 | 8 h per UTC month | `free-cf` | none |
| `standard_monthly_v2` | $20 / month | 60 standard-hours per paid period | `std-2` (2 vCPU / 8 GiB, AWS m6i.large) | **none; hard stop** |
| `compute_monthly_v2` | $100 / month | 500 standard-hours per paid period | `std-2`, `cmp-4` (4 vCPU / 16 GiB, m6i.xlarge), `cmp-8` (8 vCPU / 32 GiB, m6i.2xlarge) | opt-in, $0.25 per standard-hour, monthly cap |
| `pro_monthly_v1` | $20 / month (legacy) | honoured as sold, never sold again | `std-2` | none |

- **Weights.** A standard-hour is one hour at 2 vCPU. `std-2` = 1x, `cmp-4` = 2x, `cmp-8` = 4x. So 500 standard-hours = 500 / 250 / 125 actual hours.
- **Free.** Accounts created at or after `FREE_V2_EFFECTIVE_AT` get 2 h; older accounts keep 8 h.
  - The constant is `null` until the founder sets the release date. Until then every Free account keeps 8 h, so nothing is silently revoked.
  - The change is disclosed on /pricing when it takes effect.
- **ARM.** ARM (M7g) appears only in the economics. It is not selectable until the image, host, supervisor, editor and browser suite passes on arm64.
- **Storage.** The 60 GB workspace target is **not offered** until capability, quota and persistence are verified. The existing caps apply until then.
- **AI.** The $3 managed-AI gate stays explicit, separate and **off** (gate G4). Paid compute works when managed AI is unavailable. No $20 or $100 AI grant exists.
- **Stripe catalog.**
  - One Product per family (Standard, Compute, Compute overage).
  - Immutable Prices addressed by `lookup_key`: `ezil_standard_monthly_v2`, `ezil_compute_monthly_v2`, `ezil_compute_overage_v2`.
  - Lookup keys are resolved at runtime, so there is no per-price env var.
  - A price change is a new Price under a new key, never an edit of a sold one.
  - Clients never send amounts.

## 2. Metering (integer, authoritative)

- **Epoch.** A computer at **one** size, from **ready** (usable resource allocated) to its **verified stop**.
  - A size change closes the epoch (`resize`) and opens another.
  - Provisioning, failed provisioning and platform outages are recorded as `overheadSeconds` and never billed.
  - Instants are normalised to whole seconds in the customer's favour: `readyAt` rounds up, `endedAt` rounds down.
- **Rating a window.**
  - Clip every epoch to the window, which splits exactly at period and month boundaries.
  - Count an open epoch to `min(now, readyAt + 21,600 s)`. A closed epoch is also counted to at most `readyAt + 21,600 s`, so a missed stop can never bill past the session limit.
  - Sum `actual × weight` **before** any rounding. There is no per-heartbeat or per-minute rounding.
- **Period.**
  - `remaining = max(0, allowance − used)`.
  - `over = max(0, used − allowance)`.
  - `overageCents = floor(over / 144)`, because $0.25 per standard-hour = 1¢ per 144 std-s.
  - `billable = min(overageCents, cap)`, and only if overage is enabled.
  - **Example:** 150 h at 8 vCPU = 600 standard-hours. That is 100 h over, so $25 of overage, plus the $100 subscription, before tax.
- **Allowance.**
  - Free: the catalog value per UTC month.
  - Paid: the **ledger**, i.e. Σ grants for the period (meter `compute_std_seconds`) minus revokes and suspensions. Never a constant.

## 3. Grants, payments and plan changes

- **Period grants.** Only a **paid** invoice with `billing_reason` ∈ {`subscription_create`, `subscription_cycle`} grants a period's allowance.
  - The base-price line must be found by Price lookup key (never "first line"), and it must cover that period.
  - One grant per `(subscription, period_start)`, idempotent across replays.
- **What never grants:**
  - an overage invoice (`metadata.kind = overage`);
  - a manual invoice;
  - an unpaid renewal (it cannot refresh paid quota).
- **Upgrade (Standard → Compute).**
  1. `previewUpgrade` uses `POST /v1/invoices/create_preview`, with the proration date echoed back.
  2. `confirmUpgrade` updates the subscription item with `payment_behavior=pending_if_incomplete` and `proration_behavior=always_invoice`. The billing anchor is kept.
  3. The change applies **only** when the proration invoice is paid (`customer.subscription.pending_update_applied`).
  4. The paid proration invoice (`billing_reason=subscription_update`) grants `floor((1,800,000 − 216,000) × remaining / period)` std-s as `upgrade_prorated`.
  5. Usage carries over and nothing resets.
  6. If payment fails or needs action, the pending update stays until Stripe expires it (`…pending_update_expired`); nothing is granted.
- **Downgrade and cancel** happen at period end by default: a subscription schedule, or `cancel_at_period_end`. The Customer Portal gets an explicit `configuration` with plan switching **disabled**, so it cannot bypass pay-before-upgrade.
- **Refunds and disputes.** A refund or dispute that arrives before the grant it revokes is **retried** later, not dropped.

## 4. Overage (Compute only)

- **Opt-in.** The customer sees and accepts the rate and cap. The consent record stores the terms version (`OVERAGE_TERMS_VERSION`), rate, cap, catalog version, actor and time. No existing subscription is enrolled automatically.
- **Cap.** The default cap is $25 (2,500¢). The customer can change it within 0..$100 (`OVERAGE_MAX_CAP_CENTS`, a platform constant). At the cap, the computer saves and pauses.
- **Invoicing.** Overage is invoiced **in arrears, on its own invoice**, through the provider-neutral `UsageBillingPort`. The adapter is the Stripe invoice sidecar.
  - **Timing:** after the period closes plus a grace watermark.
  - **Lines:** one line per period using the `ezil_compute_overage_v2` Price; quantity = cents, 1 unit = 144 std-s.
  - **Idempotency key:** `overage:<subscription>:<period_start>:<rating_version>`.
  - **Invoice:** `charge_automatically`.
  - It is a separate invoice from the subscription invoice; a combined bill is not promised.
  - Metronome would be the replacement adapter, but it needs a separate commercial account, so it is not used.
- **Failure.** If an overage invoice fails or needs action (including off-session SCA), overage turns **off**. Usage stops at the allowance until the payment is repaired, and saved files are retained.
- **Pause mid-period.** Overage accrued while consent was active and within the cap is billable; overage accrued after a pause (by the customer or after a failed overage invoice) is not.
- **Known limitation.** Each run invoices only the latest closed period; an earlier period that was missed is not caught up.

## 5. Sessions and enforcement

- **Session limit.** A session is bounded at **6 h (21,600 s)** of wall-clock from ready, independent of the monthly allowance.
  - Warnings at 15 and 5 minutes before the end.
  - Then a checkpoint (flush to the durable disk), close the epoch, stop, and allow a restart.
  - A restart never resets the monthly meter.
  - Copy never implies that process memory survives a restart. Files, the editor state on disk and installed tools do survive; running processes do not.
- **Idle and long jobs.** Idle sleep after 10 minutes when genuinely idle. Running agent or terminal jobs keep consuming allowance and remain bound by 6 h.
- **Failed save.** If the snapshot or remote save fails, the durable disk is kept. The meter still closes at the deadline, so billing is never unbounded. Recovery UI is honest; it never presents an empty replacement as success.
- **Start gate** (server side):
  - `headroom = max(0, allowance + (overage on ? cap × 144 : 0) − used)`.
  - Admit only if `floor(headroom / weight) ≥ 60 s` and no other computer of the subscription is running.
  - `maxRunSeconds = min(21,600, floor(headroom / weight))`.
  - Refusal reasons: `no_plan`, `size_not_allowed`, `allowance_exhausted`, `overage_cap_reached`, `payment_issue`, `concurrent_limit`, `upgrade_pending`.
- **Paid sizes** run on AWS only. The Cloudflare class is 2 vCPU / 6 GiB, so Free stays there.
- **Delinquent paid plan** (lead decision 2026-10-18): a past_due/incomplete plan or an unresolved payment issue falls back to the Free allowance on `free-cf`; paid sizes are refused with `payment_issue`.

## 6. Router (tRPC `billing.*`)

Shapes are pinned in `contract.ts` (§ "Wire DTOs").
- `summary → BillingSummaryV2Result`
- `createCheckout({sku: SellableSku}) → CheckoutStartDTO` (Free → paid only)
- `previewUpgrade`, `confirmUpgrade`
- `scheduleDowngrade`, `cancelAtPeriodEnd`
- `setOverage`, `selectSize`
- `history → BillingInvoiceDTO[]`, with `kind` separating subscription and overage invoices
- `portal`, `checkoutStatus`

Money is integer cents, seconds are integers, and instants are ISO UTC strings.
